Artist Statment:

Creating art has always felt natural to me. I've loved making things for as long as I can remember, and creativity has always been a part of who I am. Whether I'm painting, sewing, decorating, or trying a new creative hobby, I love the feeling of getting completely absorbed in the process and losing track of time while bringing an idea to life. Acrylic paint is my favorite medium because of its versatility, although I enjoy experimenting with mixed media, oil paint, and other materials to challenge myself and continue learning new techniques. I most often work on canvas, but I'm always interested in exploring different surfaces and artistic approaches. My inspiration comes from many places—an interesting image, a landscape, a conversation, a passing thought, or something I imagine. I'm also inspired by other artists and animators whose work encourages me to think creatively. While I admire highly realistic art, I find myself most drawn to creating abstract pieces and landscapes, where I can focus on color, emotion, and imagination rather than perfect realism.

Biography:

My name is Lakeycia Cleveland, and I was born and raised in Pine Bluff, Arkansas. In 2023, I moved to Little Rock with my cat, Pudding, and currently work in the research department at the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences UAMS. Although I've always been passionate about art, I never seriously considered it as a career because of the common belief that there aren't many opportunities in the field. Instead, art became something I returned to whenever I wanted to express myself creatively. Creating this portfolio is my way of putting myself out there, sharing my work with others, and encouraging myself to create more consistently alongside my many other creative interests. My love for art was encouraged by my middle and high school art teacher, Ms. Thomas, who gave me opportunities to showcase my work in local exhibitions. In 2013, my acrylic painting The Flower Valley received a blue ribbon representing my school in the Pine Bluff Art League Middle School Art Exhibit at the Reynolds Building, and the achievement was featured in the local newspaper. During high school, I participated in two additional local art exhibitions, where my nature-themed artwork received positive recognition. I was also featured in the newspaper after recreating my school's logo.
